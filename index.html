Our Process

A considered route from the first question to long-term, measurable growth.

01 / 06

Discovery

Before we touch anything, we study your brand — your market, your customers, your competitors, your goals. We ask the uncomfortable questions most agencies skip. No flavour without understanding first.

02 / 06

Strategy

We mix data, insight, and instinct into a plan built only for you. Every brand gets a different recipe — because no two mangoes taste the same, and neither should two brands. No templates. No leftovers from someone else's project.

03 / 06

Creation

Design, content, campaigns, code — all crafted in-house, hand-blended, not assembly-lined. This is where the ideas actually get built, tested, and sharpened before anyone sees them.

04 / 06

Launch

We don't believe in "ready by Monday." We believe in ready right. Every launch is checked, tasted, and refined until it's something we'd proudly put our name on.

05 / 06

Optimise

Great brands aren't finished, they're refined. We track performance, gather data, and keep tasting, testing, and sharpening — always. Growth isn't a single bite. It's a habit.

06 / 06

Partnership

We're not a one-project agency. The best brands we've worked with, we've worked with again and again. We stick around for the next course, and the one after that.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how we work.

What makes Mangobyte different from every other agency?
Most agencies sell services. We sell flavour — the thing that makes people remember you after they've forgotten everyone else.
Why "Mangobyte"?
Because a mango isn't just sweet — it's bold, bright, and impossible to ignore. That's how we want your brand to feel. Byte, because we live in digital.
Do you work with small businesses or only big brands?
Size doesn't matter. Flavour does. If your brand has something worth saying, we'll help you say it louder.
How long does a project take?
As long as it needs — not a day more, not a day less. Good mangoes aren't rushed. Neither are good brands.
What if I don't know what my brand needs yet?
That's exactly why we start with a Taste Test. You bring the fruit. We find the flavour.
Is this just marketing, or something more?
Marketing tells people you exist. We make them care that you do. That's the bite.
Do you only take on projects in certain industries?
No. Flavour isn't industry-specific. We've worked across sectors — what matters is whether a brand is ready to be bold.
What if my brand already has an identity — can you still help?
Always. Sometimes it's not about starting from scratch, it's about finding the bite that's already there and making it louder.
Do I need all five services, or can I pick just one?
Pick what you need. Some brands need the whole mango. Some just need the zest. We tailor the mix to what actually moves your business.
How involved will I need to be in the process?
As involved as you want. We handle the heavy lifting, but the best results happen when it's a real collaboration — your brand, our craft.
What does "success" look like to you?
Not just likes and impressions. Real growth — leads, sales, recognition, and a brand people actually remember. That's the only metric that matters to us.
How do we get started?
One conversation. That's all it takes to find out if your brand's ready for its bite.
