Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how we work.

What is your typical project timeline?
Our standard timeline for a full website redesign or MVP app development is 6-8 weeks. This includes discovery, design, development, and testing. However, complex projects or enterprise solutions may require additional time. We always provide a clear, customized roadmap before starting any work.
Do you work with startups or established enterprises?
We partner with both! We help ambitious startups build their brand from scratch and scale quickly, while also assisting established enterprises in modernizing their legacy systems, improving user experience, and driving digital transformation.
What is your pricing model?
We offer custom pricing based on the scope and complexity of your project. We typically work on a fixed-bid basis for well-defined projects (like a website build), and a monthly retainer model for ongoing services like SEO, digital marketing, and continuous optimization.
How do you ensure the quality of your work?
Quality is built into our process. We conduct multiple rounds of rigorous QA testing across different devices and browsers. We also believe in transparent communication, involving you in key review milestones to ensure the final product exceeds your expectations.
